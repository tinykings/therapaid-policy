Privacy policy

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how TheraPaid handles information when you use the app. TheraPaid does not operate an external user-account system or developer-controlled server for your practice records. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to track you across apps or websites.

In short: information you enter is used to provide app features and remains under your control through your device, private iCloud database, and files you choose to export.

Information handled by TheraPaid

Depending on how you use the app, you may enter:

  • Client details: names, phone numbers, email addresses, default fees, and notes.
  • Session details: dates, fees, payment status, and notes.
  • Business expense details: dates, categories, descriptions, and amounts.
  • App preferences: invoice templates and recently used settings.

TheraPaid processes this information on your device to provide recordkeeping, summaries, invoice text, backup, and tax-estimate features. We do not receive or access this app content through a developer-controlled backend.

Sensitive information

Client and session notes may contain sensitive or health-related information if you choose to enter it. Enter only information you are authorized to store. You are responsible for determining whether your use meets professional, legal, contractual, and regulatory obligations that apply to your practice.

Storage and iCloud

TheraPaid uses Apple SwiftData and CloudKit. App records are stored on your device and, when iCloud is available, sync through your private iCloud database so they can remain available across devices signed in to your Apple Account.

Apple processes iCloud data under its own terms and privacy policy. Your iCloud settings, Apple Account status, device backups, and Apple’s retention practices can affect storage and availability. Learn more in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

TheraPaid also stores limited preferences on your device using Apple-provided storage. These preferences do not create a profile with us.

Optional purchases

TheraPaid offers optional tips through Apple’s in-app purchase system. Apple processes payment and transaction information. We do not directly receive your full payment-card details. Apple may provide transaction status and sales reporting needed to deliver, verify, and administer purchases, subject to Apple’s policies.

Sharing and data exports

TheraPaid does not sell or share app records with advertisers or data brokers. Data leaves the app only through services you direct or Apple services needed for app operation, including:

  • iCloud sync: records sync through Apple CloudKit.
  • JSON export: you may create and save or share a backup containing your app records.
  • Invoice sharing: you may send generated invoice text using apps or services you select.
  • Support email: information you voluntarily include in a support request is sent through your email provider and received by us.

Files or text you export are no longer controlled by TheraPaid. Their handling depends on the destination, people, and services you choose.

Your choices and data retention

You control the information you enter. You can edit or delete records in the app and export a JSON backup. Records synced with iCloud may remain in backups or be retained by Apple according to your settings and Apple’s policies.

TheraPaid has no developer account containing your practice records, so we cannot retrieve, correct, or delete those records for you. You can manage iCloud through your device and Apple Account settings. Before deleting the app or changing iCloud settings, export any data you want to keep.

Security

TheraPaid relies on security features provided by iOS, iPadOS, SwiftData, and CloudKit. No storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect your device and Apple Account with a strong passcode, current software, and two-factor authentication.

Children’s privacy

TheraPaid is a professional finance and practice-management tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through a developer-operated service.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as TheraPaid changes or legal requirements evolve. Updated versions will appear on this page with a revised effective date.

Need help?

For app support, privacy questions, purchase issues, or feedback, email TheraPaid support. Include your app version and device model when useful, but do not send real client records, clinical notes, or other sensitive information.
